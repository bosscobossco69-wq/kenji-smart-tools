How to use this word counter

  1. Paste or type your text into the box. The totals update as you write.
  2. Read the word, character, sentence and paragraph counts, plus the reading and speaking time.
  3. Pick a limit from the list, such as a social media post or a meta description, to see how many characters you have left.

It is useful for essays, articles, product descriptions, social posts, emails and any writing with a length limit.

What each number means

  • Words: groups of text separated by whitespace. Chinese, Japanese and Korean text is handled separately, see below.
  • Characters: every character, including spaces and line breaks. An emoji counts as one character.
  • Characters without spaces: the same count with all spaces and line breaks removed.
  • Sentences: an estimate based on common ending marks such as periods, question marks and exclamation marks, including the full-width marks used in Chinese and Japanese.
  • Paragraphs: blocks of text separated by one or more blank lines.

Chinese, Japanese and Korean text

Many word counters split text only at spaces, so a whole line of Chinese is counted as one word. This tool detects Chinese, Japanese and Korean characters and counts them as words using your browser’s built-in word segmentation. If your browser does not support it, each character is counted instead. Results can differ from other programs, because there is no single agreed way to split these languages into words.

Reading and speaking time

Reading time assumes about 230 words per minute and speaking time about 130 words per minute. These are rough averages, so treat them as estimates. People read and speak at different speeds, and the difficulty of the text matters too.

Frequently asked questions

Is my text uploaded?

No. Counting is performed locally in your browser and the text is not sent to our server.

Why is my count different from Microsoft Word or Google Docs?

Tools treat hyphens, symbols, numbers and non-English text differently, so counts can differ slightly. If a strict count is required, check the final number in the program that will receive your text.

Do spaces count as characters?

The main character count includes spaces and line breaks. The more-details section also shows the count without spaces.

Why are sentence counts only estimates?

Abbreviations, initials and different languages use punctuation in different ways, so the tool estimates sentences from common ending marks. Check the text by hand when an exact number matters.

Are the platform character limits exact?

They are common guideline figures. Platforms change their limits, and some search results are truncated by pixel width rather than characters, so confirm the current limit on the platform you use.
