How the conversions work

Each unit is defined by how many base units it contains, such as meters for length or kilograms for weight. To convert, the tool changes your value into the base unit and then into the unit you chose. Where an exact definition exists, such as 1 inch = 2.54 centimeters or 1 pound = 0.45359237 kilograms, the tool uses it. Results are shown to about 12 significant digits, so long decimals are rounded for display.

Temperature

Temperature scales do not simply multiply, because their zero points differ. The tool uses these formulas:

  • Celsius to Fahrenheit: °F = °C × 9 ÷ 5 + 32
  • Fahrenheit to Celsius: °C = (°F − 32) × 5 ÷ 9
  • Celsius to Kelvin: K = °C + 273.15

Values below absolute zero (−273.15 °C) are not possible, and the tool will tell you if you enter one.

Units that are easy to mix up

  • US and UK gallons and pints are different. A US gallon is about 3.785 liters and an imperial (UK) gallon is about 4.546 liters. Both are included and labeled.
  • MB and MiB are not the same. Storage makers usually use decimal units, where 1 GB is 1,000 MB. Many operating systems use binary units, where 1 GiB is 1,024 MiB. Both sets are included so you can see the difference.
  • Tons: a metric tonne is 1,000 kilograms, while a US short ton is 2,000 pounds.
  • Year: the time converter uses a year of 365 days. Months are left out because their length varies.

Frequently asked questions

How many centimeters are in an inch?

There are exactly 2.54 centimeters in an inch. This is the international definition, so the value is exact and not rounded.

Are US and UK gallons the same?

No. A US gallon is about 3.785 liters and an imperial (UK) gallon is about 4.546 liters. The same difference applies to pints and fluid ounces, so choose the correct version.

What is the difference between MB and MiB?

MB uses decimal units, where 1 MB is 1,000,000 bytes. MiB uses binary units, where 1 MiB is 1,048,576 bytes. The same difference applies to KB and KiB, GB and GiB, and TB and TiB.

How do I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

Multiply the Celsius value by 9, divide by 5, then add 32. For example, 20 degrees Celsius is 68 degrees Fahrenheit.

How accurate is the converter?

It uses exact definitions wherever they exist. Results are displayed to about 12 significant digits, so very long decimals are rounded for display.

Does the converter store or upload my values?

No. Conversions happen in your browser and nothing is sent to our server.
