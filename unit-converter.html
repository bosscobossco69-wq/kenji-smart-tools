Convert length, weight, and temperature

Use the three independent converters to switch between common measurement units. Enter a value, select its current unit, and choose the unit you want. Results update automatically in your browser.

Length units

Convert millimeters, centimeters, meters, kilometers, inches, feet, and miles. For example, 1 meter equals 100 centimeters or approximately 39.3701 inches.

Weight units

Convert milligrams, grams, kilograms, metric tons, ounces, and pounds. For example, 1 kilogram is approximately 2.20462 pounds.

Temperature units

Convert Celsius, Fahrenheit, and Kelvin. The converter uses the standard formulas between these temperature scales.

Conversion examples and reference values

For length, 1 kilometre equals 1,000 metres, and 1 metre equals 100 centimetres. For weight, 1 kilogram equals 1,000 grams. These relationships make it easy to check whether a result is in the expected range before using it.

Celsius and Fahrenheit

Temperature conversion uses a different formula from length and weight. To convert Celsius to Fahrenheit, multiply by 9/5 and add 32. To convert Fahrenheit to Celsius, subtract 32 and multiply by 5/9. For example, 0°C equals 32°F, while 100°C equals 212°F.

Choose the correct unit

Check whether a value refers to a metric or customary unit before converting it. A pound is a unit of mass, while a US fluid ounce is a unit of volume; they are not interchangeable without knowing the substance's density. Similarly, a US gallon and an imperial gallon are different sizes.

Precision and practical use

Displayed results may be rounded for readability. For cooking, travel, schoolwork, or general reference, a rounded result is often sufficient. For engineering, laboratory, medical, or regulated work, confirm the conversion factor, required precision, and applicable standard with an authoritative source before relying on the result.
