Calculate age from a date of birth

Enter a date of birth in month/day/year format to calculate age in completed years, months, and days. The tool also estimates how many days remain until the next birthday using your device’s local date.

How the age calculation works

The calculator compares the birth date with today’s date, accounting for whether the birthday has already occurred this year and borrowing days from the previous month when needed. Dates in the future and invalid calendar dates are rejected.

Frequently asked questions

Which date format should I enter?

Use MM/DD/YYYY, for example 07/31/2000. The format is shown beside the date field.

Is my date of birth uploaded?

No. The calculation runs in your browser and the date is not sent to our server.
