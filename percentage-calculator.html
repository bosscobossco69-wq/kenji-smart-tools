Three ways to calculate percentages

This free percentage calculator handles common everyday calculations: finding a percentage of a number, finding what percentage one number is of another, and calculating the percentage change between two values.

1. Find a percentage of a number

Multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. Example: 15% of 200 is 30.

2. Find what percentage one number is of another

Divide the first number by the second, then multiply by 100. Example: 30 is 15% of 200.

3. Calculate percentage change

Subtract the starting value from the new value, divide by the starting value, then multiply by 100. A positive result is an increase; a negative result is a decrease. The starting value cannot be zero.

Percentage examples for everyday calculations

Percentages express a part of a whole out of 100. To calculate a 20% discount on RM150, multiply 150 by 0.20: the discount is RM30, so the sale price is RM120. To find what percentage RM45 is of RM300, divide 45 by 300 and multiply by 100: the answer is 15%.

Understand percentage increase and decrease

If a value rises from 80 to 100, the change is 20. Divide 20 by the original value of 80 and multiply by 100 to get a 25% increase. If it falls from 100 to 80, the change is -20, which is a 20% decrease. The direction matters because percentage change uses the starting value as its reference.

Common percentage mistakes

Do not confuse percentage points with percent change. An interest rate moving from 4% to 5% increases by one percentage point, but the relative increase is 25%. Also remember that a percentage change from a starting value of zero is undefined, because division by zero is not possible.
