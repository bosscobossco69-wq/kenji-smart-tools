Case Converter

Convert text between UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case, Sentence case and code styles such as camelCase and snake_case. Your original text is never replaced.

How to use the case converter

  1. Paste or type your text into the first box.
  2. Pick a case. The converted text appears in the result box straight away.
  3. Copy the result, or use it as the new input to convert it again.

Your original text stays in the input box, so you can try several cases and compare them.

How Title Case works

Title Case here capitalizes the first letter of every word and lowercases the rest. Style guides such as AP, APA, Chicago and MLA disagree about small words, so there is an option to keep short words like a, the, of and and in lowercase. The first and last word are always capitalized. Check the result against the style guide you follow.

Code styles and URL slugs

For camelCase, PascalCase, snake_case, kebab-case, CONSTANT_CASE and URL slugs, each line is converted separately, so you can paste a list of names and convert them all at once. These cases split words at spaces, punctuation and capital letters, so XMLHttpRequest becomes xml_http_request in snake_case.

A URL slug uses only the letters a to z, digits and hyphens. Accents are removed, so Café becomes cafe. Characters from other scripts, such as Chinese, are dropped.

Things to check after converting

  • Acronyms and brand names such as NASA or iPhone lose their capitals in Title Case and Sentence case.
  • Sentence case lowercases everything else, including names. It does restore the single word I.
  • Some letters change length. In German, the letter ß becomes SS in uppercase.

Frequently asked questions

Is my text uploaded?

No. Conversion happens in this page in your browser and the text is not sent to our server.

Does the converter change punctuation?

UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case, Sentence case, aLtErNaTiNg and iNvErSe keep your punctuation and spaces. The code-style cases (camelCase, PascalCase, snake_case, kebab-case, CONSTANT_CASE) and URL slugs remove punctuation and spaces and join the words.

Why did Title Case or Sentence case lowercase my acronyms and names?

These cases lowercase the rest of each word before capitalizing, so acronyms such as NASA and names such as iPhone need to be fixed by hand. Sentence case does restore the single word I.

Will the original text be replaced?

No. Your text stays in the input box and the converted text appears in the output box, so you can try another case without losing the original.

Does it work with Chinese or other languages?

Chinese, Japanese and Korean have no upper and lower case, so those characters are left unchanged. Scripts with case, such as accented Latin, Greek and Cyrillic, work with the case conversions. URL slugs keep only a to z and digits, so characters from other scripts are removed.
