Browser-based tools

Tools such as the Word Counter, Case Converter, JSON Formatter, Password Generator, calculators, and Unit Converter perform their main calculations in your browser. Text, passwords, dates, and values entered into these tools are not intentionally sent to our application server by those tools.

AI Customer Reply Generator

If you use the AI Customer Reply Generator, the customer message and the options you select are sent from our server to an AI service provider to generate a reply.
