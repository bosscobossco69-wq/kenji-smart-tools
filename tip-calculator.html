How to use the tip calculator

  1. Enter the bill amount before tax.
  2. If your bill has a tax or service charge, enter its percentage. Leave it at 0 if not.
  3. Choose a tip percentage with the quick buttons or type your own.
  4. Enter how many people are splitting the bill. The tool shows what each person pays.

How the tip is calculated

  • Tax or service charge = bill × tax % ÷ 100
  • Tip = base × tip % ÷ 100, where the base is the bill before tax or the bill including tax, whichever you choose
  • Total = bill + tax or service charge + tip
  • Each person pays = total ÷ number of people

Amounts are rounded to the nearest cent at each step, which is how a printed bill usually works.

Tipping, taxes and service charges

Tipping customs differ widely by country, and in some places it is not expected at all. In the United States, 15% to 20% is a common range for table service. Many restaurants elsewhere, including in Malaysia, add a service charge and a sales and service tax (SST) to the bill. Check your receipt before adding a tip, since a service charge may already cover it.

To include these charges, type their combined percentage into the Tax or service charge box. If one charge is applied on top of the other, the exact total can differ slightly, so compare it with your receipt.

Splitting the bill

The tool divides the total equally between everyone. It does not split by individual items. If the total does not divide evenly, each share is rounded to the nearest cent, so the shares may differ from the total by a cent or two. The rounding option rounds each share up to a whole number, which makes the bill easier to pay in cash. The extra amount ends up as additional tip.

Frequently asked questions

Should I calculate the tip on the bill before or after tax?

There is no single rule. Many people tip on the amount before tax, while others tip on the total including tax. The tool lets you choose either one.

How do I include a service charge or tax?

Type its percentage into the Tax or service charge box. If your receipt shows more than one charge, enter the combined percentage as an estimate and compare the total with your receipt, because one charge may be applied on top of the other.

How does the tool split the bill?

It divides the total equally between the number of people you enter. It cannot split by individual items.

What does rounding up each share do?

Each person pays the next whole number above their exact share. The total paid is slightly higher than the bill total, and the difference is effectively extra tip.

What tip percentage should I use?

It depends on local custom and the service you received. A common range for table service in the United States is 15% to 20%, while in many other countries tipping is smaller or optional. Check the usual practice where you are.

Does the tool store my numbers?

No. The calculation happens in your browser and nothing is sent to our server.
