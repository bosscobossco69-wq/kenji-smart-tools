04 / privacy overview

Your inputs stay close.

WhatsApp links, invoice calculations, and image resizing run in your browser. QR codes are generated with a client-side library. Most tools run directly in your browser without an account. The AI Customer Reply Generator sends the message you submit to an AI service to create a reply. Advertising and hosting providers may also process technical information.
